## HIGH-YIELD DRUG INTERACTIONS (EMI-tested)
- **Fluvoxamine** (#1 interaction drug): CYP1A2 inhibitor. +diazepam → accumulation. +theophylline → 3× levels (dose 1/3). +warfarin → 98%↑ PT.
- Carbamazepine: CYP3A4 inducer
- Lithium + NSAIDs/thiazides → toxicity
- MAOI + SSRI → serotonin syndrome
- MAOI + tyramine → hypertensive crisis

## TERATOGENICITY (recall-confirmed, high yield)
| Drug | Effect |
|---|---|
| Valproate | Spina bifida (10×), anencephaly, heart defects (4×), cognitive impairment. Most teratogenic. 7.2% any defect. |
| Lithium | Ebstein's anomaly (tricuspid valve) |
| Carbamazepine | Spina bifida ~0.5%, rash 10-15% |
| Lamotrigine | SJS (slow titration!) |
| Paroxetine | Neonatal irritability, pulmonary hypertension |

## TOXICITY MANAGEMENT (EMI-tested)
- Lithium (tremor/ataxia) → stop, IV fluids, haemodialysis if severe
- Methadone (pinpoint pupils) → naloxone IM (short-acting, monitor re-emergence)
- MAOI crisis (cheese/wine) → phentolamine or chlorpromazine
- Barbiturate OD → alkalinise urine
- Amphetamine OD → acidify urine

## NON-PSYCHIATRIC DRUG PSYCHIATRIC EFFECTS (unique question type)
- Levodopa → visual hallucinations, psychosis
- Digoxin → green halos, confusion
- Corticosteroids → hypomania, confusion
- Interferon-α → depression
- Beta-blockers (lipophilic) → depression
- Isoniazid → psychosis
- Mefloquine → psychosis, anxiety

## 3. NEUROSCIENCES: EXAM-SPECIFIC DETAILS

### Structures SPMM tests that general textbooks miss
- **Planum temporale**: Larger on LEFT in 65%. Most asymmetrical brain structure. Reduced/reversed in schizophrenia.
- **Insula**: Deep in Sylvian fissure. PRIMARY GUSTATORY CORTEX.
- **Papez circuit**: Hippocampus→fornix→mammillary bodies→mammillothalamic tract→anterior thalamus→genu of IC→cingulate→parahippocampal→entorhinal→perforant pathway→hippocampus
- **Alexander's 5 BG circuits**: Motor, Oculomotor, DLPFC (executive), Anterior cingulate (motivation), Orbitofrontal (social)
- **Inferior olivary nucleus**: Contralateral ataxia (finger-nose). NOT same as cerebellar (ipsilateral).
- **Recurrent artery of Huebner**: ACA branch, caudate head. Lesion → agitated→akinetic+abulic.
- **Fahr's disease**: Basal ganglia calcium. Early onset → schizophreniform + catatonia. Late → dementia + choreoathetosis.

### Blood supply lesions
- ACA: Bilateral → quadriparesis (legs>arms) + akinetic mutism
- MCA: Contralateral hemiplegia + aphasia/neglect
- PCA: Hemianopia, alexia without agraphia
- PICA: Wallenberg's (vertigo, ipsilateral face/Horner/9-10 palsy, contralateral spinothalamic loss)
- AComm: Akinesia + personality change + confabulatory amnesia (like Wernicke-Korsakoff)

### CSF route
Lateral→Foramen of Monroe→3rd→Aqueduct of Sylvius→4th→Foramen of Magendie (single) + Luschka (2 lateral)→subarachnoid. 300ml/day.

## 4. STATISTICS & CRITICAL REVIEW (50 MARKS — HIGHEST YIELD)

### Formulas (practise until automatic)
- NNT = 1/ARR. ARR = CER - EER. NNT = 1/(CER-EER). ROUND UP.
- RR = EER/CER. RR<1 = benefit, crosses 1.0 = not significant.
- OR = (a×d)/(b×c). Used in case-control studies (no new cases).
- Sensitivity = TP/(TP+FN) = SnOUT (high sensitivity rules OUT)
- Specificity = TN/(TN+FP) = SpIN (high specificity rules IN)
- PPV = TP/(TP+FP) — depends on prevalence
- NPV = TN/(TN+FN) — depends on prevalence
- LR+ = sensitivity/(1-specificity). RECALL: LR+ = 4 when sens=80%, spec=80%.
- Standard error of mean = SD/√n

### Cost types (recall-tested)
- Direct: drug cost, equipment, staff time
- Indirect: sick leave, lost productivity
- Intangible: pain, stigma

### Susser's causation criteria
Time order is the ONLY must. Others: consistency, specificity, predictive performance, strength.

### Statistical tests
- Continuous normal: t-test (2 groups), ANOVA (3+), Pearson (correlation)
- Continuous skewed: Mann-Whitney (2 groups), Kruskal-Wallis (3+), Spearman (correlation)
- Categorical: Chi-square (unpaired), McNemar (paired), Fisher's exact (small cells)
- Survival: Kaplan-Meier + log-rank test

### Forest plot interpretation
- Square size = study weight (n size)
- Horizontal line = 95% CI (crosses 1.0 = not significant)
- Diamond = pooled estimate
- I²: <25% low, >75% high heterogeneity. High I² = studies too different to pool.
- Funnel plot asymmetry = publication bias

### Randomisation types
- Simple, Block (equal group sizes), Stratified (by covariates), Minimisation (next patient to smaller group)

## 5. RECALL-CONFIRMED HIGH-YIELD CLINICAL PATTERNS

### Know these exact presentations
1. "Weight gain caused by antipsychotics" = 5-HT2C antagonism
2. "GI discomfort caused by SSRIs" = 5-HT3 stimulation
3. "Seizures + antidepressant" = Bupropion >400mg
4. "Ovarian cyst + mood stabiliser" = Valproate (PCOS, RR 7.5)
5. "Green halos + heart failure + confusion" = Digoxin toxicity
6. "Target lesions + blistering + bipolar" = Lamotrigine SJS
7. "Amylase elevated + abdominal pain + valproate" = Pancreatitis
8. "Clozapine + tachycardia + high troponin" = Myocarditis (saddle ECG)
9. "Donepezil increased + hypotensive + bradycardia" = Sinus bradycardia
10. "Jittery baby + overaroused" = Maternal opioid (not just cocaine)
11. "Microcephaly + growth retardation" = Fetal alcohol syndrome
12. "Newborn + cleft palate + heart defect + dev delay" = Valproate
13. "Pick's + no insight + language" = Primary motor dysphasia
14. "Short + freckles + Lisch nodules + LD" = Neurofibromatosis type 1
15. "Noisy kid + severe LD + hypertelorism" = Cri du chat
16. "MECP2 + female + regression 6-18mo + hand-wringing" = Rett syndrome
17. "Chronic ketamine + bladder pain + haematuria" = Ketamine cystitis
18. "Schizotypal vs schizoid" = Schizotypal has ANXIETY (schizoid = aloof, indifferent, solitary)
19. "Autism favours schizophrenia if" = Near normal development (autism has delay), mood incongruent (not typical)
20. "Which one favours autism?" = Ritualistic behaviours (more typical of autism than schizophrenia)

### Cognitive distortions from recall
- "He had bad intentions when I saw him" = Mind reading / emotional reasoning
- "He only said it because he corrected the slide" = Discounting the positives
- "One nice meal = I'll be obese" = Dichotomous thinking
- "I got an A but that doesn't mean I'm intelligent" = Minimisation

### Defence mechanisms (ICD-11 emphasis)
- Borderline PD: Splitting (idealisation/devaluation) + Acting out
- Schizotypal: Persistent anxiety + social anxiety

## 6. PSYCHOTHERAPY: EXAM LEVEL

### Modality selection
- Bulimia → CBT-ED. Anorexia (adolescent) → Family therapy (Maudsley). BPD + self-harm → DBT. First-episode psychosis → Family intervention. Mild depression → low-intensity CBT. Moderate-severe → high-intensity CBT + meds. Depression relapse → MBCT.
- IPT focuses: Grief (complicated bereavement), Role dispute (conflict), Role transition (life changes), Interpersonal deficits (social isolation)
- DBT structure: Weekly individual (1hr) + group skills (2hr) + telephone coaching + therapist consultation team
- DBT pretreatment: Motivation and agreements. Priority in DBT: Self-harm.
- MI elements: Cognitive dissonance amplification, express empathy, roll with resistance, support self-efficacy, goal setting

### Key figures
Beck=CBT, Linehan=DBT, Ryle=CAT, Minuchin=family structural, Haley=family strategic, Bowlby=attachment, Freud/Klein/Winnicott=psychodynamic

## 7. CHILD & ADOLESCENT PSYCHIATRY

### ADHD management by age
- <5: Parent training only. NO medication.
- 5-18: Methylphenidate first-line → lisdexamfetamine → guanfacine/atomoxetine
- ADHD + mild LD: Lisdexamfetamine (atomoxetine second-line — lower efficacy)
- ADHD cutoff age: <12 (ICD-11/DSM-5)

### Autism interventions (exam-tested)
- Visual schedule → Now-and-then cards (NOT social stories)
- Social stories = for social understanding (e.g., "why mum visits")
- Intensive interaction = imitate their body language and sounds
- EIBI, TEACCH = educational
- NO medication for core symptoms
- Autism + anxiety: Social anxiety CAN be diagnosed in ASD — high-functioning ASD/Asperger experience more anxiety

### Other
- OCD in children: Most common comorbidity = depression (NOT GAD in kids — GAD in adults)
- Body dysmorphic disorder in adolescents: Increased suicide/self-harm risk (NOT benign, NOT self-limiting)
- Mild depression in child: Watchful waiting 2 WEEKS (NOT 2 months — common distractor)
- 8yo depression: Fluoxetine only SSRI licensed for children
- School refusal vs truancy: Refusal = anxiety (parent knows). Truancy = concealment + antisocial.
- Anorexia + diuretic/laxative abuse + lethargy = hypokalaemia (U wave, long PR on ECG)

## 8. FORENSIC & LEGAL

### Testamentary capacity (DON'T confuse with MCA capacity)
Must know: extent of property, who the beneficiaries are, nature of the will. NOT just "no dementia."

### Children Act
- S17: Services for children in need. S47: Investigate significant harm.
- EPO: 8 days + 7 extension. Care order: until 18.
- Gillick: Under 16 can consent if sufficient understanding. Fraser = contraceptive advice.

### MCA + children
MCA does NOT apply to under-16s. For 16-17: MCA applies but modified by Children Act.

### Recall patterns
- Infanticide most common in: Psychosis
- Sexual reoffending strongest predictor: Sexual deviancy + antisocial PD
- Dangerous driving: Mania, dementia, antisocial PD
- Victims of female homicide: Mostly <16
- Diagnostic overshadowing: Attributing new Sx to existing LD, missing treatable condition
- Most common early onset dementia: Alzheimer's (NOT FTD)
- MCI vs dementia: Functional impairment from collateral is key

## 9. CRITICAL REVIEW WORKED EXAMPLES

### LR+ calculation
Sens=80%, Spec=80% → LR+ = 0.80/(1-0.80) = 0.80/0.20 = 4.0

### NNT from 2×2
CER=25%, EER=10% → ARR=15% → NNT = 1/0.15 = 6.7 → ROUND UP to 7

### Why OR in case-control?
No new cases — you select by disease status. Cannot calculate incidence.

### Intention-to-treat
Patients who agree then withdraw before treatment: include in original group (ITT analysis). Do NOT exclude — this preserves randomisation.

### Quality assessment tools
- PRISMA = systematic reviews
- CASP = critical appraisal (valid? results? relevant?)
- Risk of Bias = RCT quality

### Single most important audit step
Collect data → present → implement change → re-audit. Missing: implementation of change.
